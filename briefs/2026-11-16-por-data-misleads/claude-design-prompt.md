# Claude Design prompt: Rental Intelligence Suite carousel (Point of Rental)

Build a 7-slide social carousel. Use the Point of Rental design system (Rebrand 2026). Use ONLY the text and art in this file. Do not write new copy, add statistics, change quote wording or add imagery.

## Canvas and system
- 1080 x 1350 px (4:5), 7 slides, light off-white background, dark text, brand green as the highlight.
- Margins 72 px. Headline at least 64 px bold. Support text at least 30 px. Nothing smaller than 24 px.
- Every slide: the "Rental Intelligence Suite" lockup top left (use the design system's asset, do not redraw it) and a 7-segment page indicator top right with the current slide filled.
- Slides 1 to 6: a "SWIPE →" cue bottom right (24 px minimum). Slide 7: no cue.
- Highlight the key phrase of each headline with a brand green underline, as listed below.
- No buttons or button-like shapes. No logos other than the lockup. No stock photos. No extra illustration.

## Art (files in the art/ folder)
All art is transparent PNG, 16:9 or 2:1. Place it as a strip in the lower 40% of the slide at the full content width (936 px), keep clear space above it for type, keep at least 24 px between the art and the support text, and never stretch, recolor, redraw or crop the dots.
- Slide 5's art (05-how-it-works.png) has empty space on its left. If the strip looks lopsided, you may trim only the empty transparent area on the left.
