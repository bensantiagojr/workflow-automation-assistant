# Claude Design prompt: Thanksgiving post (Record360)

Make one single-image social post. Use the Record360 design system. Type only. Use ONLY the text in this file. Do not write new copy.

## Canvas and system
- 1080 x 1350 px (4:5), one slide.
- Use only colors, fonts and the logo lockup from the design system. Do not add orange, brown, gradients or other autumn colors unless they are in the design system.
- Type only: no illustration, no icons, no stars, no photos, no vehicles, no clip-art, no emoji. The design system's own background shapes or patterns are allowed if they are part of its standard layouts, kept subtle.
- Warm and professional. Generous empty space. Let the type carry the holiday.

## Text (exact)
Headline, large, left aligned: "Happy Thanksgiving."
Support line under it: "Thank you to our customers and partners. Wishing you a safe and happy holiday."
Logo: the Record360 lockup from the design system (including "by Point of Rental" if the lockup has it), placed in a corner or at the bottom (do not redraw it).

Highlight the word "Thanksgiving." in the headline with the brand highlight style (for example the red underline used in the Record360 carousels), if the design system uses one.

## Type sizes
- Headline at least 120 px, bold.
- Support line at least 36 px.
- Nothing smaller than 24 px.
- Margins at least 72 px.
- No buttons, no URLs, no other text.

## Export
- PNG at 1080 x 1350 named thanksgiving-r360-2026.png.

## Check before you hand back
- Text matches this file character for character, including the period after "Happy Thanksgiving."
- Colors and fonts come from the design system, with no autumn palette added.
- There is exactly one "Happy Thanksgiving" headline and no extra decorative text.
- Nothing is cut off, and the margins are 72 px.
