# Examples (original prompts these templates were built from)

These are the originals. They predate the clarification that the "graphic showing ..." line is a couple of elements pulled from the email, and that every image is clean, not busy, and has no logos. The templates in `SKILL.md` now add those lines.

## Pointillism header icon (IC26 session sign-ups)

```
I love this style a lot can you make one again different focus. All I want is pretty much a graphic showing someone booking their seats for a session, maybe floating UI elements and nice movement in the graphic. 1:1 is the aspect ratio, png transparent background. No words or numbers needed

Subject line: Session sign-ups are open - reserve your seats

Body copy: You are registered for IC26 - now it is time to build your personal agenda. Individual sessions are open for sign-up, and the most popular ones fill quickly. Log in and reserve your seats so you don't miss the sessions you are most excited about.
```

## Banner (Elite RMS refresh)

```

All I want is pretty much a graphic showing equipment and event and tent rental software upgrading its look and feel, maybe floating UI elements and nice movement in the graphic. Professional, clean vibes. 3:1 is the aspect ratio, off white background. No words or numbers needed. No need to show all the features, pick a couple to represent easily:

That's why we've invited a select group of Elite customers to review some of the more significant changes before they're released. Their feedback is helping us refine designs, catch issues early, and make sure each update makes your daily work easier.

* Your data, settings, and history stay exactly where they are
* The core workflows your team relies on will work the same way, even where the screens look different

We'll release the updates in phases, not all at once, so your team can adjust gradually. Before each phase, we'll let you know which areas are changing and share resources to help your staff get comfortable, such as release notes, short videos, webinars, and Knowledge Base articles.
What you need to do now
Nothing yet. Watch for our update communications, read all of the upcoming release notes, and please make sure the right people at your company are on our mailing list.
```

## Where the copy lives in Asana (observed)
- The email request is a parent task in the "Email Calendar" project. The graphic task ("Create email graphic design" or "Email Graphic") is a subtask of it.
- Syrinx Conference emails: "Provide a description of request" links one shared Google Doc, "Customer Emails Copy: Syrinx Conference 2026", with one numbered `## ` section per email. Each section has a metadata table (Template, From, Audience, Suppress, Subject, Prev. Text), then Main Headline, Mini-paragraph, Copy, CTA, and Email From.
- IC26 emails (for example "Reminder to register for classes"): the subject line and body copy are written directly in the task description.
