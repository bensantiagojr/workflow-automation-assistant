## Pattern
- Tension, then proof: "Was this already there?" "Let the record settle it." "With proof, they pay. Not you."
- Practical and checklist-like when teaching: "Four shots every return needs."
- Direct sentences about disputes, damage, claims and returns. Short supporting lines that name the evidence (timestamp, geotag, signature, photos).
- Human-in-the-loop framing for AI: "AI flags. People decide."

## Products and terms
Record360, 360AI, AI Damage Detection (Glass Detect, Tire Detect, Body Detect), Voice Inspect, Task Triggers, customer eSign, custom inspection forms. Use names as the source page does.

## Claims
Treat numbers as claims that need a source row: "65% better damage claim recovery" and "cut damage disputes in half" appear in earlier samples with no source recorded. Do not reuse them until Bryan confirms the source.

## Calls to action
"See Record360 in action", "Book a demo", record360.com. Say where the link is in text. No drawn buttons.

## Proof rule
Never show AI-generated photos or invented app screens as if they were real Record360 captures. Use real captures, real app screenshots or an obvious illustration.
