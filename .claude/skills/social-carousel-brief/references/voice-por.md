# Point of Rental voice

Seeded from the sample carousels (Rental Intelligence Suite, Elite, Essentials, POR One, robot series). Replace or extend with the Point of Rental design-system summary when it is exported. Michael Peck reviews copy for every post.

## Pattern
- Short declarative headlines that end in a period, often two beats: "Arrival windows, no phone call." "Signal lost. No problem." "This one hasn't moved in a month."
- Second person. Talks to the person at the counter, in the yard or in the back office.
- Plain words from the rental floor: counter, yard, fleet, dispatch, contracts, kits, utilization, idle, maintenance, disposition.
- Outcome first, product second. The headline is the problem or the result. The product shows up in the supporting line.
- Numbers only when sourced ("400+ built-in reports", "60 seconds").

## Products
Elite, Elite Cloud, Expert, Syrinx, Essentials, POR One, Rental Intelligence Suite (AI Insights, Fleet Advisor, Voice Agent, Disposition Agent). Use the names exactly as the source page does. Do not describe a feature the source page does not describe.

## Calls to action
"See Point of Rental in action", "Book a demo", pointofrental.com. On LinkedIn and Instagram say where the link is (comments or bio) in text, not as a drawn button.

## Avoid
Anything on the slop checklist, plus mascot-speak that talks down to the reader and claims about AI that the source page does not make.
