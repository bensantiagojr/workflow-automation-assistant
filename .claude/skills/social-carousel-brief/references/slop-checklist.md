# Slop checklist

Run every draft through this before it goes to review. Fix, do not flag and move on.

## Copy
- No empty intensifiers: unlock, game-changer, revolutionize, seamless, supercharge, elevate, next-level, cutting-edge, robust, leverage, "in today's fast-paced world", "it's not just X, it's Y".
- One idea per slide. A slide that needs "and" in its headline is two slides.
- No lists of exactly three added for rhythm. Use as many items as the source has.
- No more than one em dash per caption.
- No claim without a source row in the claims table.
- Use the specific noun from the source (counter, yard, windshield, hour meter) over a general one (solution, workflow).
- Numbers are copied exactly from the source, with the unit.
- The headline says what the reader gets or what goes wrong, not what the product is.
- Closing line is plain: say what to do and where. Avoid "Insight unlocked", "Get to the point".

## Visual
- No stock-looking people, no generic handshake or lightbulb imagery.
- No logos, words or numbers inside generated art (text is added in the layout).
- No readable UI text inside generated art. Show interface as abstract shapes.
- Illustration style matches the brand file. Mixed styles in one carousel are a fail.
- Real product screenshots only for product proof, never recreated by AI.
- Photoreal AI imagery is not used for damage, product proof, people or events.

## Layout
- Headline at least 64 px, body at least 30 px, nothing under 24 px at 1080 px wide. Margins at least 72 px.
- Slide 1 has the hook. Last slide says what to do in text, not as a button graphic that cannot be clicked.
- Page indicator and a swipe cue on every slide except the last.
